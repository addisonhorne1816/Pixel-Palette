What Is Pixel Palette?

Your starting point for exploring graphic and web design.

Pixel Palette is a beginner-friendly graphic and web design toolkit created to help people explore their creativity and learn the basics of design.

Whether you are creating your first website, experimenting with colors, or looking for design inspiration, Pixel Palette provides helpful resources to get you started.

Our goal is to make learning about graphic and web design simple, enjoyable, and accessible to everyone.
