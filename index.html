Beginner Design Tips

01

Start with a small palette

Try using three to five colors so your design feels coordinated instead of crowded.

02

Make text easy to read

Choose text and background colors with enough contrast, especially for smaller text.

03

Keep the layout consistent

Use similar spacing, heading styles, and button designs across your pages.
