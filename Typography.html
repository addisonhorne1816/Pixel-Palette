The Art of Typography

Fonts do more than display words. They help communicate personality, create visual interest, and make designs easier to understand.

Start with the basics

What Is Typography?

Typography is the art of arranging text to make information clear, readable, and visually appealing.

Why Does Typography Matter?

The fonts you choose can change how people feel about a design. A bold font can make something feel energetic, while a simple font can create a clean and professional appearance.

Good typography helps organize information, guide visitors through a page, and make content comfortable to read.

Explore different styles

Types of Fonts

Fonts are grouped into different categories. Each category has its own appearance and purpose.

Aa
01 / CLASSIC

Serif

Serif fonts have small decorative strokes at the ends of letters. They often create a traditional, elegant, or formal appearance.

Examples: Playfair Display, Georgia, Times New Roman

Aa
02 / MODERN

Sans Serif

Sans serif fonts do not have decorative strokes at the ends of letters. They often look clean, simple, and modern.

Examples: Arial, DM Sans, Helvetica

Aa
03 / CREATIVE

Script

Script fonts are designed to resemble handwriting or calligraphy. They can add a personal, decorative touch.

Examples: Dancing Script, Pacifico, Allura

Aa
04 / BOLD

Display

Display fonts are decorative and designed to attract attention. They work well for titles, headings, and special designs.

Examples: Oswald, Bebas Neue, Anton

Make your fonts work together

The Art of Font Pairing

Font pairing means combining two or more fonts that complement one another in a design.

PAIRING 01

Creative Ideas

Playfair Display + DM Sans

A classic serif heading paired with a simple sans serif font creates an elegant and balanced look.

Elegant Balanced
PAIRING 02

MAKE IT BOLD

Oswald + DM Sans

A strong display heading combined with a clean body font creates a modern and energetic design.

Modern Bold
PAIRING 03

A Creative Touch

Dancing Script + DM Sans

A decorative script heading with a readable sans serif font creates a friendly and personal style.

Friendly Playful

Design Tip: Try limiting your design to two or three font families. Too many different fonts can make a design feel crowded and inconsistent.

Beginner Typography Tips

Keep these simple tips in mind when choosing fonts for your next design project.

01

Keep It Readable

Choose fonts that are easy to read. Avoid overly decorative fonts for long paragraphs.

02

Create a Hierarchy

Use different heading sizes and weights to help visitors understand which information is most important.

03

Use Consistent Fonts

Keep your font choices consistent throughout your website to create a unified appearance.

04

Check Your Contrast

Make sure your text color stands out against the background so visitors can read it comfortably.
